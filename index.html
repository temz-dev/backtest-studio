How it works

The rules — same for every run

  • $10,000 starting equity, long-only spot. No leverage, no shorting.
  • 0.1% fee on every buy and every sell — and on buy-and-hold's entry and exit too.
  • Signals are computed on the daily close and executed at that close. No peeking at tomorrow.
  • 30-bar warmup. Every strategy and the benchmark start at the close of bar 30, so the comparison is fair.
  • Win rate counts completed round trips where the sell price was above the buy price.

Backtests are not predictions. A strategy that won over the last two years can lose over the next two. Nothing here is financial advice — this tool exists so you can see, honestly, what a simple rule would have done on real history.
